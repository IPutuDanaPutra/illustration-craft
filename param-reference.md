# Parameter reference (allowed values)

Not part of the schema itself — reference only, for filling in `illustration-schema.json`.

## param1_color
- Hex color (e.g. `#D5AFF2`)
- Or a color name

## param2_visual_style
3D render, vector illustration, watercolor painting, pencil sketch, cartoon style, pixel art, paper cut, low poly, high poly detail, flat design, abstract expressionism, surrealism, pop art, minimalist, futuristic, retro 80s, cyberpunk, steampunk, isometric, line art

## param4_lighting_mood
soft studio lighting, dramatic spotlight, natural sunset light, mysterious moonlight, futuristic neon lighting, soft bokeh light, volumetric lighting (god rays), rim lighting, ambient occlusion lighting, high contrast light, even lighting, warm light, cool light, underlighting, top-down lighting, global illumination simulation, glowing light, reflective light, flat lighting (no shadows), gradient lighting

## param5_background_composition
transparent background, soft gradient background, colorful abstract background, textured background (e.g. wood, stone), bokeh background, minimalist solid background, geometric pattern background, subtle illustration background, blurred nature scene background, blurred cityscape background, center-focus composition, rule-of-thirds composition, symmetrical composition, diagonal composition, close-up composition, wide shot composition, composition with additional blurred props, atmospheric composition (fog), composition with generous negative space, dynamic composition

## param6_negative_prompt
blurry, out of focus, rough texture, realistic, photo, excessive detail, odd proportions, compression artifacts, noise, distortion, faded colors, bad lighting, harsh distracting shadows, cluttered background, irrelevant elements, logo or watermark, text, cropped image, overly perfect symmetry (too rigid), too simple
